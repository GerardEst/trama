A few loose ends.

Can I start for free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free, so you can build and share your first stories before deciding whether you need more room.

Do readers need an account?

No. Share a link and readers can jump straight into your story without signing up or installing anything.

Can I export or self-host my stories?

Stories live on Trama and are shared with a link. For studios and authors who want to host or sell them on their own platform, we’re preparing a one-time Export License.

What can I create with Trama?

Interactive fiction, branching adventures, quizzes and other choose-your-own-path experiences. Start with a scene and see where it leads.
