Trama: write interactive fiction without coding

Trama (trama.app) is a web editor for branching interactive stories, choose-your-own-adventure books and text adventures. Authors write scenes on a visual board and connect them with choices. Stories can track stats, conditions and the player's own answers, lock choices behind requirements, and branch at random or by condition. Authors test every path while they write, revise as they play, and share the finished story with a link that readers open in any browser.

The editor is available in English, Spanish and Catalan.
